mandatory bound Map charge receipt and actual request-guard execution. Includes

unreleased Map `0034targetbudget` and explicit owner policy configuration. Default
calls stay unchanged. No refunds, worker, duration budget or universal network/RPS
claim. See [ADR 0029](../docs/adr/0029-cumulative-target-attempts.md).
